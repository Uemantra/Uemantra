### 🚀 What I'm about

I'm on a mission to teach myself to code again — and instead of "hello world,"
I jump straight into building **playable worlds**. Half mad scientist, half kid
with a crayon box, I'd rather make something fun and learn the hard parts along
the way than read another tutorial. I am *not* a pro (yet!), but I make up for it
with relentless curiosity.

### 🛠️ Things I love to build

- ☢️ **Tactical RPGs** with that grimy Fallout feel — turn-based combat, branching dialogue, choices that matter
- 🐱 **Couch & LAN multiplayer chaos** — yes, including artillery with kittens
- 🗡️ **Roguelikes & dungeon crawlers** — procedurally generated, one-more-run energy
- 🖖 **Sci-fi UIs & retro tech** — because every project is better with a little LCARS
- 📜 **Text adventures & little engines** — interactive worlds you build by typing
